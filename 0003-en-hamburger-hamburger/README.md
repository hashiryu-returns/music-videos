📝 Behind the Scenes
This was a full-automation experiment raced against a deadline. I asked Gemini to proofread the lyrics, but it generated a complete song, so I ran with it.

OiiOii's full-auto mode took the audio and planned and rendered all 18 scenes with Seedance. Unlike the hands-on French video, there were no stills and no storyboard of my own. A few hours, and about ¥2,000.

Not bad, not outstanding, and I still had to watch every second for breakage. As an anniversary gift, it feels the lightest, because the least of my own work went into it.
