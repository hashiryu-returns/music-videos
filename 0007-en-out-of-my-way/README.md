# EN — "Out of My Way!" (working title)

Our daughter's own video. The hedgehog from [0002](../0002-fr-je-marrete-pas/README.md) is the hero: small, bossy, sweet, in a red cape. This one is a space opera, so there's no magic. She carries a red energy sword, and when she points her paw, a fleet she commands fires down from orbit. Every robot and monster she beats pops into bubbles and floats away. At sunset she rides home to the panda, the fox and Mama, and falls asleep. Fast like 0002, because that's the one she dances to.

## Picture

Space opera, not Star Wars. No named characters, and no `Star Wars` or `Jedi` anywhere. Prompts say `lightsaber`, because Vidu draws a proper one from that word. Titles and descriptions say `energy sword`.

Rules, the same as 0006:

- **Never inside a ship.** A cockpit or a corridor becomes a room that has to stay the same. Ships are seen from outside.
- **The fleet appears once,** in the bridge, with her standing on the hull of the flagship. Every other strike from orbit shows only the beam coming down through the clouds.
- **Every enemy is used once, and no background is used twice.** No cut has to match another cut's set. Defeats are beams and bubbles over empty ground; close-ups of her are against open sky.
- **She is the only recurring character.** Omni Reference on [`hedgehog.png`](characters/hedgehog.png) in Midjourney V7, the same as 0002. Papa, her brother and Mama appear once each, in Verse 3. One character per frame. About half the cuts don't have her at all: enemies, beams, planets.
- **Nobody walks.** Feet on the ground slide in Vidu, so she leaps, lands, sits, rides the hoverbike in #3, or acts standing still.
- **Beams and bursts stay out of the still.** Vidu adds them from the motion line. Bubbles that are already floating can be painted in.

**Cut length.** Takes land on the sung line breaks, which sit on the 172 BPM bar lines. Where two lines follow the same subject they're one 8s take with two actions (Vidu Q2 in Off-Peak Mode is free at any length), so the camera holds while the action keeps moving. Single-line takes run 3.1–5.0s. Nothing flickers by in 1–2s.

## Suno

Custom Mode, model v6, Vocal Gender **Female**, Duration Auto, Weirdness 40, Style Influence 70, no Persona.

Style:

```text
anime opening theme, fast J-rock, English female vocal, bright playful powerful voice, 172 BPM, tight distorted guitars, driving bass, punchy live drums, hand claps, gang vocal shouts on the hook, call and response chant in the bridge, bright synth stabs, key lift into the last chorus, fun bossy and triumphant, polished modern Japanese rock production
```

Exclude:

```text
nursery rhyme, children's song, lullaby, baby voice, kids choir, chiptune, 8-bit, video game music, orchestral trailer, musical theatre, broadway, brass fanfare, pop punk, metalcore, screaming, growl, male vocal, rap, ballad, slow, lo-fi, country
```

It's for a five-year-old, but she picked 0002 because it's real fast J-rock. A kids' song sound would lose that, so `nursery rhyme`, `children's song` and `baby voice` are excluded. The childishness lives in the lyric, not the arrangement.
