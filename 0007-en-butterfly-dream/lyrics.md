## Suno

Custom Mode, the same model as 0006, Vocal Gender **Female**, Duration Auto, Weirdness 40, Style Influence 70, no Persona.

The sound is a slow, melancholic wa-rock ballad: about 96 BPM, D minor, quiet at the start, building chorus by chorus to the loudest stretch near the end, then a soft fade. Koto and shakuhachi carry the quiet parts, the band and taiko come in for the choruses, and the woman's voice has a folk singer's vibrato and ornaments on the held notes. Not a high-tone belt. Each instrument has its own place, since a bare list gets one or two of them.


Exclude:

```text
Japanese lyrics, enka, fast, upbeat, guitar solo, heavy distorted guitar, shamisen, piano, strings, synth, 80s retro, chiptune, video game music, musical theatre, broadway, opera, high soprano, pop punk, metal, screaming, growl, male vocal, rap, EDM, lo-fi, country
```

The voice is described, not named: an artist's name in the Style is refused. It is a shigin singer's way of singing: a strong chest voice rather than a high belt, notes that swell as they are held, a wide vibrato at the end of each phrase and kobushi turns between notes, soft in the verses and full in the chorus.

The koto and the shakuhachi lead the Style, since Suno weighs the first tags most, and each has its place: the koto under the verses, the shakuhachi answering the voice, a solo each in the interlude. The shamisen, piano and strings are excluded so nothing else sits in their range, and the guitars stay down with no solo.

`enka` stays excluded although the vibrato is wanted: a slow minor ballad with a folk vibrato is where Suno would reach for it, and the track has to stay rock. If the ornaments don't come, move `kobushi` to the front of the Style.

The first Style asked for a mid-tempo driving rock beat and excluded `ballad` and `slow`, and gave the opposite song.

## Butterfly Dream

A woman who is a butterfly and a magician performs for whoever is watching, and every door she opens is another world: a Japanese room of paper screens, a Chinese palace of red silk and jade, a golden theatre, a European palace of white lace and chandeliers. Each world is a new costume and a new trick, and in the morning the theatre is bare. The riddle of the old butterfly dream runs under it: was she ever there. Not a love song.
