# 0006 lyrics — drafts

Four drafts. Pick one, or take one draft's chorus and another's story. Every draft is built the same way: verses 1 and 2 are the everyday half of the picture, the pre-chorus is the turn, and from verse 3 on it's the fight.

| Draft | The fight | Borrowed from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steel in Bloom | Two rivals who share tea every morning, then a duel on a bridge in the rain | rival swordswomen of the same town |
| Last Night of Swords | Blades are banned. One woman took a badge and a gun; the other hid her sword under the floor | the sword ban, and the last stand of blades against rifles |
| Under One Word | Sworn sisters under one banner. Leave and you die. One leaves, and the other is sent after her | the sword corps' one code, where deserting meant death |
| Red Lantern Run | Two women side by side, storming a lantern-lit inn up the stairs. Not a one-on-one | the night raid on an inn, fought on its stairs |

No names, eras, places or Japanese terms. `sword`, `blade`, `lantern`, `bridge` and `sandals` are fine.

## Suno

Exclude:


`wa-rock` and the Japanese instruments pull Suno toward Japanese vocals, so `Japanese lyrics` is in Exclude.

Listing the instruments gave a shamisen-only track: Suno picks one or two from a list. Each instrument now has a place where the guitars leave room for it. The shakuhachi got lost in a sung bridge, so it opens the intro and has the `[Interlude]`, the only stretch of the song with no vocal. `symphonic metal` added strings that sat in the same range as the koto and shakuhachi, so it's `power metal` instead.

## Under One Word
