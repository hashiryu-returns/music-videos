# EN — working title "Two Blades"

A sword fight between two women, with no message to it. The point is two cool characters and a fight worth watching. The first half shows their everyday lives and where they come from; the second half is all fight.

- **Status:** lyric drafts. Pick one in [`lyrics.md`](lyrics.md), then roll it in Suno
- **Setting:** today or the near future. If it's the near future, a world like Ghost in the Shell
- **Sound:** J-rock with Japanese instruments, a high-tone female vocal, a metal edge
- **Source:** the end of the samurai era, the fall of the old government and the sword corps that fought for it. The source only gives texture: no names, eras, places or Japanese terms in the lyric, the same rule as [0004](../0004-en-not-alone/README.md)
- **Tools:** a test of Vidu Q4 for fluid motion, Seedance/MiniMax style. Cut in Final Cut Pro, not iMovie
