# 0004 Not Alone — sibling songs

Three songs were written on one story, the first Dragon Quest, each with its own sound. Not Alone is the only one that got an MV, and its lyric is in [README.md](README.md#lyrics). The other two are here. Their lyrics are locked and neither v2 style has been rendered. No MV is planned for either.

| Song | Sound | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not Alone | cinematic anime fantasy, female choir | shipped |
| Half of Everything | anime opening, fast J-rock | not made; its v2 style is the base of [0005](../0005-en-out-of-my-way/README.md)'s sound |
| Bring the Morning Home | celtic folk rock | not made |

Each one follows the same source ([Kamigame spoiler summary](https://kamigame.jp/%E3%83%89%E3%83%A9%E3%82%AF%E3%82%A81/%E6%94%BB%E7%95%A5%E3%82%AC%E3%82%A4%E3%83%89/%E3%83%8D%E3%82%BF%E3%83%90%E3%83%AC.html)), with no names or game terms in the lyric. For both songs here, the Suno settings are Custom Mode, model v6, Vocal Gender **Female**, Duration Auto, Weirdness 40, Style Influence 70, and no Persona.

## Half of Everything

An anime opening. One traveller, a world gone grey, a bridge made of rain and sun, and an offer of half the world refused twice. Fast, cool, and still bright.

### Suno

Style:

```text
anime opening theme, fast J-rock, English female vocal, powerful clear belted chorus, 175 BPM, tight distorted guitars, driving bass, punchy live drums, bright string stabs, piano runs in the pre-chorus, key lift into the last chorus, triumphant and cool, polished modern Japanese rock production
```

Exclude:

```text
chiptune, 8-bit, video game music, orchestral trailer, musical theatre, broadway, marching band, brass fanfare, pop punk, metalcore, screaming, growl, male vocal, rap, spoken word, ballad, lo-fi, country
```

To hear a male take, change `female` to `male` in Style, swap `male vocal` for `female vocal` in Exclude, and set Vocal Gender to Male. Change nothing else, so the two takes compare.

### Rejected style v1

```text
fast driving heroic J-rock, English male vocal, 150 BPM, bright brass hits over distorted-but-clean electric guitar, soaring strings in the chorus, punchy drums, clipped confident verses, a shouted-sung hook that stays major and triumphant, cool defiant energy, polished current production
```

It came out dated and lame. Brass hits and `heroic` pull it toward a movie trailer, and v1 used the same orchestra and male vocal as its two siblings, so all three sounded alike. v2 leads with a modern genre (`anime opening theme`), drops brass, keeps strings as colour, and switches to a female belt.

## Bring the Morning Home

The journey itself. Town lamps, a long road, a warning at a bolted door, a bridge where rain meets sun, and the colour coming back to the fields. Field music you walk to.

### Suno

Style:

```text
celtic folk rock, English female vocal, bright clear voice, driving reel rhythm, fiddle lead, tin whistle melody, bouzouki and acoustic guitar strumming, bodhrán with full drum kit, warm bass, big open singalong chorus, wind and wide fields, hopeful and adventurous, modern polished production
```

Exclude:

```text
chiptune, 8-bit, video game music, renaissance faire, sea shanty, pirate, drinking song, irish pub, country, bluegrass, banjo, male vocal, orchestral trailer, musical theatre, marching band, brass, dark, grim
```

To hear a male take, change `female` to `male` in Style, swap `male vocal` for `female vocal` in Exclude, and set Vocal Gender to Male.

### Rejected style v1

```text
heroic orchestral adventure, English male vocal, bright marching tempo, soaring strings, bold brass fanfare, woodwinds, snare and timpani, lyrical verses opening into a triumphant sung chorus, hopeful courageous major key, polished cinematic production
```

Snare, timpani, brass fanfare and `marching` make a parade band, and `heroic orchestral` with a male lead lands on musical theatre. v2 takes the sound of JRPG field music instead: folk instruments over a rock rhythm section, with a female lead.
