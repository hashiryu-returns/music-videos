# EN — "Out of My Way!"

Our daughter's own video. She watched [0002](../0002-fr-je-marrete-pas/README.md) over and over, singing and dancing, and kept going back to the opening, where the hedgehog (her) comes in. She likes a fast song, so this one is 172 BPM, and the lyric is simple enough for a kid to sing. She's the hero of an adventure: small, bossy, sweet, in a red cape. This one is a space opera, so there's no magic. She carries a red energy sword she can throw like a boomerang, and her satellite base fires a laser when she needs it, twice in the whole song: at the walker in the first chorus and at the robot squid in the last. Robots and monsters either run, burst into bubbles, or shatter into ice. At sunset she rides home to the panda, the fox and Mama, then lies down.

## Picture

Space opera, not Star Wars. No named characters, and no `Star Wars` or `Jedi` anywhere. Prompts say `lightsaber`, because Vidu draws a proper one from that word. Titles and descriptions say `energy sword`.

Rules, the same as 0004:

- **Never inside a ship.** A cockpit or a corridor becomes a room that has to stay the same. Ships are seen from outside.
- **The fleet appears once,** in the bridge, flying past her as she stands on a rock above the clouds.
- **The laser from orbit fires twice,** at the walker (#10–#11) and the squid (#41). Every other enemy she beats herself or scares off. A satellite seen from orbit never came out, so the strike is shown from above.
- **Every enemy is in one take, and no background is used in two takes.** No take has to match another take's set. A split take plays as two cuts, and the halves match because they're the same clip. Defeats are shown over empty ground with the enemy out of the shot; close-ups of her are against open sky.
- **She is the only recurring character.** Omni Reference on [`hedgehog.png`](characters/hedgehog.png) in Midjourney V7, the same as 0002. Papa, her brother and Mama appear once each, in Verse 3. One character per frame. About half the cuts don't have her at all: enemies, beams, planets.
- **Nobody walks.** Feet on the ground slide in Vidu, so she leaps, lands, sits, rides the hoverbike in #3, or acts standing still.
- **Beams and bursts stay out of the still.** Vidu adds them from the motion line. Bubbles that are already floating can be painted in.

**Cut length.** Takes land on the sung line breaks, which sit on the 172 BPM bar lines. Where two lines follow the same subject they're one 8s take with two actions (Vidu Q2 in Off-Peak Mode is free at any length), so the camera holds while the action keeps moving. Single-line takes run 3.1–5.0s.

**Split takes.** Seven clips play twice (cuts 3, 6, 7, 10, 16, 21 and 25): the first action is one cut, an insert follows, and the second action comes back after it. That is 36 clips and 43 cuts. From the bridge on, every cut is its own generation. The insert is a different subject, never her in the same situation: the other side of a fight, or the world reacting (#4, #13). Nothing in it has to match the take it splits. Split halves and inserts can run 1.5–2.1s; they sit on one sung phrase each.

Cut numbers are iMovie timeline slots, and each made clip and still is named after the first cut that uses it (`clips/25.mp4`, `stills/29.png`). Section times come from `captions/en.vtt`. 36 clips make 43 cuts. Three second halves were exported as their own files (8 of 6, 9 of 7, 18 of 16), so the folder has 39 mp4s. The other four have no file of their own: 5 (of 3), 12 (of 10), 23 (of 21), 28 (of 25). Times chain from 0:00 on the shot lengths. Transitions sit on the joins and are not added in. iMovie's project length reads 02:26. The cuts chain to 2:20.0, the fade ends at 2:21.0, and the last caption ends at 2:21.8.

No take shares a background with another, except two pairs of Vidu clips from one still: #22 and #24, #26 and #27. Each enemy is in exactly one take; the walker, the drones and the giant each show up twice by being split. Defeats happen over empty ground with the enemy out of the shot (#10–#13 for the walker, #25–#31 for the dragon and the jelly), or inside the enemy's own clip (#41, the squid). Close-ups of her are against open sky, so there's nothing behind her to match. The only thing carried between takes is the planet's sky and time of day: desert day (#1–#18, #34), ice planet at dusk (#19), the same planet at night (#25–#31), swamp at night (#20), crystal canyon at midday (#21–#24), above the clouds (#32–#33), desert at night (#39), desert at sunset (#35–#38), pink sea in the morning (#40–#42), orbit (#43).

Only her, the family and the lightsaber recur. Omni Reference holds one thing, so anything else she wears or rides is in one take: the hoverbike and goggles are only in #3 and its second half, #5.

## Suno

Custom Mode, model v6, Vocal Gender **Female**, Duration Auto, Weirdness 40, Style Influence 70, no Persona.

Style:

```text
anime opening theme, fast J-rock, English female vocal, bright playful powerful voice, 172 BPM, tight distorted guitars, driving bass, punchy live drums, hand claps, gang vocal shouts on the hook, call and response chant in the bridge, bright synth stabs, key lift into the last chorus, fun bossy and triumphant, polished modern Japanese rock production
```

Exclude:

```text
nursery rhyme, children's song, lullaby, baby voice, kids choir, chiptune, 8-bit, video game music, orchestral trailer, musical theatre, broadway, brass fanfare, pop punk, metalcore, screaming, growl, male vocal, rap, ballad, slow, lo-fi, country
```

It's for a five-year-old, but she picked 0002 because it's real fast J-rock. A kids' song sound would lose that, so `nursery rhyme`, `children's song` and `baby voice` are excluded. The childishness lives in the lyric, not the arrangement.
