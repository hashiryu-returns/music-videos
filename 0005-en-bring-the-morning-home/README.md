# EN — "Bring the Morning Home" (working title)

The journey itself. Town lamps, a long road, a warning at a bolted door, a bridge where rain meets sun, and the colour coming back to the fields. Field music you walk to.

- **Status:** lyrics locked, style v2 not yet rendered
- **Source:** the story of the first Dragon Quest ([Kamigame spoiler summary](https://kamigame.jp/%E3%83%89%E3%83%A9%E3%82%AF%E3%82%A81/%E6%94%BB%E7%95%A5%E3%82%AC%E3%82%A4%E3%83%89/%E3%83%8D%E3%82%BF%E3%83%90%E3%83%AC.html)). No names or game terms in the lyric.
- **Siblings:** [0004](../0004-en-half-of-everything/README.md) and [0006](../0006-en-not-alone/README.md).

## Lyrics

```text

[Verse 1]
The morning at the gate is thin
The fields remember being green
They waited on the castle stair
And closed a promise in my hand

A stranger spends one sentence, then
I thank them and I take the road
It's longer than the map I drew

Where the rain meets the sun
A bridge across the grey

I'm walking it back today

[Outro]
```
